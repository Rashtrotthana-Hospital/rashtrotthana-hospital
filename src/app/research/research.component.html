About The Rashtrotthana Research Centre
The Rashtrotthana Research Centre stands as a pioneering institution dedicated to integrative medical research, merging various branches of healthcare including Biomedicine, Ayurveda, Homeopathy, Naturopathy and Yoga.
Through comprehensive integrative research, it aims to explore diverse domains of healthcare delivery, validate treatment modalities, practices and lifestyle modifications.
Embracing a multidimensional approach, this unique methodology is geared towards enhancing the well-being of humanity and fostering a healthier nation.

OUR RESEARCH FOCUS
Key Areas of Research
Advancing integrative healthcare through scientific inquiry, validation, and community impact.
Pre-clinical and Clinical research
  • Preventive and therapeutic research – Bio-medicine and Ayush systems.
  • Integrative research - identify domains where synergy and complementarity is possible.
  • Bridging the treatment gaps-Focus on diseases or conditions where in the conventional system has unmet medical need.
  • Preclinical research in collaboration with Chanakya University and other organizations.
Healthcare research
  • Standardization and validation of treatment protocols.
  • Aiming for mainstream applicability of Ayush systems and policy advocacy.
  • Development of Research ideas based on the analysis of data and core principles of Ayurveda.
Community health Research
  • Impact studies, cohorts to generate evidence for lifestyle and traditional practices through research modules.
  • Dissemination of such knowledge in the community.
  • Evidence based community health enhancement through development of products and outreach programmes.
  • Community health programmes targeting specific populations such as women health, geriatric health, child health etc.
  • Public engagement for health promotion.
