Multispeciality Hospital in RR Nagar RR Nagar Bangalore.

Jayadev Memorial Rashtrotthana Hospital & Research Centre, a 162-bed Integrated Multi-Specialty Tertiary Care Hospital, is recognized as the best multispeciality hospital in RR Nagar Bangalore, offering world-class healthcare facilities. As part of the Rashtrotthana Trust, we provide expert, holistic care in Modern Medicine, Ayurveda, Homeopathy, Yoga and Naturopathy. Our renowned doctors and specialists ensure comprehensive, patient-centered treatment while maintaining affordability and advanced care solutions.
The guiding force behind Rashtrotthana Parishat, Sri M. C Jayadev, envisioned, nurtured and mentored the organization from its inception. As the embodiment of duty and nationalism, his life-long commitment to 'One Life, One Mission' was unparalleled. Rashtrotthana Parishat continues to work towards building a prosperous and healthy society, founded on the strong, enduring principles laid by him. Today, the hospital stands as one of the best multispeciality hospital in RR Nagar Bangalore, contributing to society's well-being through world-class healthcare.
